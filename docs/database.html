Database Schema

PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy 2.0 models, Alembic migrations. ~30 tables, almost all tenant-scoped.

Core models by area

Tenancy & identity

ModelNotes
BusinessThe tenant root. Not itself tenant-scoped — it is the tenant.
StoreA physical location under a business. Most tenants have exactly one.
UserStaff account. email unique per-business, not globally.
CustomerDiner account. Can be a login-capable account or a staff-entered CRM record with no login (password_hash null).

Catalog & inventory

Product, Category, Brand, UnitCatalog. Products carry both retail fields (SKU, cost, tax rate) and restaurant/menu fields (description, allergens, calories, is_available_online).
InventoryCurrent stock per product per store.
StockMovementFull audit ledger of every stock change — sale, purchase, adjustment, transfer, return — with reason and reference.

Orders & sales

Order, OrderItemCovers both in-store POS sales and online orders. fulfillment_type null = in-store; set = pickup/delivery/dine-in, with its own fulfillment_status lifecycle (pending → confirmed → preparing → ready → completed).
Refund, RefundItemItem-level returns — restocks exactly what's returned, computes the amount from the order's own recorded prices, never a client-supplied number.
DiscountPercentage or fixed, with min-subtotal and date-window rules, validated server-side only.
HeldSaleA parked cart, resumable from any terminal.

Restaurant-specific

RestaurantTableDine-in tables — status (free/occupied/reserved), QR-code ordering target.
ReservationTable bookings, guest or logged-in customer.
ReviewOne per (product, customer) — enforced by a unique constraint, gated to customers who actually ordered the item.
NewsletterSubscriberEmail opt-in with an unsubscribe token (globally unique — a capability token, not tenant data).
NotificationLogEvery email attempted, with real delivery status once Resend is configured.

Purchasing & finance

SupplierVendor records.
Purchase, PurchaseItemStock received — increments inventory, logs a StockMovement.
PurchaseReturn, PurchaseReturnItemReturns to a supplier.
PurchasePaymentAccounts-payable tracking — partial/full payments against a purchase invoice.
StockTransferMoves stock between two stores of the same business.
ExpenseBusiness expenses, feeds the profit/loss report.

Audit

AuditLogWho did what, when — logged centrally through one shared log_action() helper called from every mutating endpoint.

The tenancy pattern

Every table above (except Business itself) carries a business_id foreign key, added by the multi-tenancy migration. See Multi-Tenancy for how it's enforced at query time.

Recurring migration gotchas worth knowing

  • ALTER TYPE ... ADD VALUE on a Postgres enum can't run inside a transaction — needs with op.get_context().autocommit_block():.
  • sa.Enum(...) used via op.add_column doesn't auto-create the type the way op.create_table does — needs an explicit .create(op.get_bind(), checkfirst=True) first.
  • Adding a NOT NULL column to a non-empty table needs the nullable-then-backfill-then-SET NOT NULL two-step (or a server_default for simple constants like booleans).
