About Us

Since 1998, Bavaria Genuss has served traditional Bavarian dishes — Schnitzel, Knödel, and Gulaschsuppe — made from local, seasonal ingredients. Whether you're stopping by for lunch, celebrating with family, or ordering delivery to the office, we've got a table (or a box) for you.
